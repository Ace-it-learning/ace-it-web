# DSE English Micro-Skills Taxonomy

## Overview
Comprehensive breakdown of all micro-skills tested in HKDSE English Language exam, organized by paper and skill category. This taxonomy enables bottom-up skill tracking and targeted improvement.

**Total Micro-Skills: 47**

---

## Paper 1: Reading (12 micro-skills)

### Comprehension Skills (6)
1. **Literal Comprehension** - Understanding explicit information
2. **Inference** - Reading between the lines, implied meanings
3. **Main Idea Identification** - Identifying central themes and arguments
4. **Detail Recognition** - Locating specific facts and data
5. **Sequencing** - Understanding chronological order and cause-effect
6. **Synthesis** - Combining information from multiple sources

### Critical Reading Skills (6)
7. **Fact vs Opinion** - Distinguishing objective from subjective statements
8. **Author's Purpose** - Identifying intent (persuade, inform, entertain)
9. **Tone & Attitude** - Detecting writer's stance and emotions
10. **Register & Style** - Recognizing formal/informal language
11. **Metaphorical Language** - Understanding figurative expressions
12. **Text Organization** - Recognizing structure (compare/contrast, problem/solution)

---

## Paper 2: Writing (15 micro-skills)

### Content & Ideas (3)
13. **Relevance** - Staying on topic and addressing the prompt
14. **Development** - Elaborating ideas with examples and details
15. **Originality** - Presenting unique perspectives and insights

### Language & Vocabulary (5)
16. **Vocabulary Range** - Using varied and precise words
17. **Collocations** - Natural word combinations (make a decision, not do a decision)
18. **Idiomatic Expressions** - Appropriate use of idioms and phrases
19. **Register Appropriateness** - Matching formality to context
20. **Word Choice Precision** - Selecting exact words for meaning

### Grammar & Sentence Structure (4)
21. **Sentence Variety** - Mix of simple, compound, complex sentences
22. **Advanced Structures** - Inversion, cleft sentences, conditionals
23. **Grammatical Accuracy** - Correct tenses, subject-verb agreement
24. **Punctuation** - Proper use of commas, semicolons, etc.

### Organization & Coherence (3)
25. **Paragraph Structure** - Topic sentences, supporting details, conclusions
26. **Transitions** - Smooth connections between ideas (however, moreover)
27. **Overall Coherence** - Logical flow from introduction to conclusion

---

## Paper 3: Listening (10 micro-skills)

### Comprehension Skills (5)
28. **Main Idea Listening** - Grasping overall message
29. **Detail Listening** - Catching specific information (names, dates, numbers)
30. **Note-Taking** - Recording key points accurately
31. **Prediction** - Anticipating what comes next
32. **Listening for Gist** - Understanding general meaning without every word

### Advanced Listening Skills (5)
33. **Accent Recognition** - Understanding different English accents
34. **Speed Processing** - Handling fast speech rates
35. **Speaker Attitude** - Detecting emotions and opinions from tone
36. **Integrated Tasks** - Combining listening with writing/speaking
37. **Ambiguity Handling** - Interpreting unclear or indirect statements

---

## Paper 4: Speaking (10 micro-skills)

### Fluency & Delivery (4)
38. **Pronunciation Clarity** - Clear articulation of sounds
39. **Intonation** - Natural rise and fall of voice
40. **Pace & Rhythm** - Speaking at appropriate speed with natural pauses
41. **Confidence & Naturalness** - Speaking without excessive hesitation

### Interactive Skills (3)
42. **Turn-Taking** - Knowing when to speak and listen
43. **Active Listening** - Responding relevantly to others
44. **Facilitation** - Encouraging others to contribute

### Language Use (3)
45. **Spontaneity** - Thinking on feet, improvising responses
46. **Vocabulary in Speech** - Using varied words naturally
47. **Grammatical Accuracy in Speech** - Correct structures while speaking

---

## Skill Levels for Each Micro-Skill

Each micro-skill is rated on a 7-point scale:
- **Level 1**: Unclassified (no evidence of skill)
- **Level 2**: Basic (minimal competence)
- **Level 3**: Adequate (simple but correct)
- **Level 4**: Good (clear and accurate)
- **Level 5**: Strong (varied and effective)
- **Level 5***: Excellent (nuanced and precise)
- **Level 5****: Exemplary (sophisticated and flawless)
