# Official HKDSE English Language Assessment Criteria Summary

> **Note**: This summary is derived from standard HKDSE English Language Assessment Frameworks. Please verify with specific 2025 updates if available.

## General Grade Descriptors (Subject-Wide)

| Level | Descriptor Summary | Key Keywords |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 5\*\*** | **Exemplary Performance** <br> Demonstrates sophisticated and appropriate use of language. Ideas are complex, well-developed, and insightful. Processing of information is rapid and accurate. | *Sophisticated, Insightful, Flawless, Expert* |
| **Level 5\*** | **Excellent Performance** <br> Demonstrates strong and accurate use of language with occasional minor errors. Ideas are well-organized and clearly expressed. | *Excellent, Nuanced, Precise* |
| **Level 5** | **Strong Performance** <br> Demonstrates a wide range of vocabulary and sentence structures. Communication is clear and effective. | *Strong, Varied, Effective* |
| **Level 4** | **Good Performance** <br> Language is generally accurate. Understands main ideas and details. Expression is clear but may lack sophistication. | *Good, Clear, Accurate* |
| **Level 3** | **Adequate Performance** <br> Basic understanding of texts. Simple language is used correctly. Can communicate main points but with some errors. | *Adequate, Basic, Simple* |
| **Level 2** | **Basic Performance** <br> Understanding is partial. Simple sentences with frequent errors. Relies on keywords/repetition. | *Partial, Weak, Fragmented* |
| **Level 1** | **Unclassified** <br> Minimal understanding. Isolated words only. Communication is not achieved. | *Minimal, Irrelevant, Memorized* |

## Paper-Specific Breakdown

### 1. Reading (Paper 1)

* **Level 5** vs **Level 4**:
    * **Level 5**: nuanced understanding of writers' attitudes, identifying implied meanings (inference), distinguishing fact from opinion with high accuracy.
    * **Level 4**: good understanding of explicit info, can identify main ideas, but may struggle with subtle tone or complex inference.

* **Criteria for 5\* / 5\*\***:
    * **5\***: Ability to synthesize information from multiple parts of the text; answers are concise and precise.
    * **5\*\***: Flawless comprehension of complex texts; strong ability to interpret metaphorical language and register; answers demonstrate high analytical capability.

### 2. Writing (Paper 2)

* **Level 5** vs **Level 4**:
    * **Level 5** ("Sophisticated"): Varied sentence structures (inversion, relative clauses), precise vocabulary (collocations), strong coherence (smooth transitions), stylistic awareness (genre-appropriate tone).
    * **Level 4** ("Clear"): Grammatically correct simple/compound sentences, appropriate vocab (though less varied), logical paragraphing, generally appropriate tone.

* **Criteria for 5\* / 5\*\***:
    * **5\***: Engaging content, original ideas, impressive range of vocab used naturally.
    * **5\*\***: "Native-like" command; creativity in expression; highly persuasive or impactful writing; virtually error-free.

### 3. Listening (Paper 3)

* **Level 5** vs **Level 4**:
    * **Level 5**: Captures specific details and names accurately; understands speakers' attitudes/feelings; correct spelling/grammar in data file tasks.
    * **Level 4**: Understands main points and most details; some spelling/grammar errors that don't impede meaning; identifies general attitude.

* **Criteria for 5\* / 5\*\***:
    * **5\***: Ability to interpret ambiguity; handles fast speech rates; high accuracy in synthesizing information for integrated tasks.
    * **5\*\***: Perfect transcription of complex info; adept at identifying irony or subtle stance; high-quality written production in Part B tasks.

### 4. Speaking (Paper 4)

* **Level 5** vs **Level 4**:
    * **Level 5**: Sustains conversation naturally; actively engages others; wide range of vocab/sentence patterns; clear pronunciation/intonation.
    * **Level 4**: Maintains interaction; accurate but less varied language; generally clear pronunciation; responds relevantly.

* **Criteria for 5\* / 5\*\***:
    * **5\***: Leadership in discussion (facilitates without dominating); insightful contributions; improvises effectively.
    * **5\*\***: Charismatic delivery; highly sophisticated language; responds sharply to complex ideas; natural and "effortless" fluency.

## Grading Criteria for Ace It Calibration

To align with the official framework, our AI assessment will focus on:

1.  **Complexity**: Is the student using simple *S-V-O* sentences (Lvl 3) or complex structures (Lvl 5)?
2.  **Precision**: Is the answer vague ("It is bad") or precise ("It detrimentally impacts...")?
3.  **Depth**: Does the answer skim the surface (Lvl 4) or provide insight/inference (Lvl 5)?
4.  **Accuracy**: Are there grammatical errors? (Lvl 5 must be nearly error-free).
